# RelayOps engineering exercise

You've joined a team maintaining an internal ticket-triage console. The application works, but users have reported inconsistent queue behavior. The automated suite also contains a failing regression test.

Spend the first few minutes exploring the product and repository. Run the tests, reproduce what you observe, and investigate the data flow. Identify high-impact correctness problems and implement focused fixes. Refactor where it helps, while keeping the scope manageable.

During the session, explain your observations, hypotheses and trade-offs. Verify your changes with useful tests and manual checks, and finish by describing any remaining concerns. You may consult documentation and ask clarifying questions.

Plan for 60–90 minutes. Prioritize a well-supported diagnosis and a small, verified improvement over a large rewrite.
