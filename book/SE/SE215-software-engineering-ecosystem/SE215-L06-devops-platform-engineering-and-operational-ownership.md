### Learning Objective

Understand the operational organization surrounding software delivery and which responsibilities remain with application engineers.

### Opening Problem

Developers say their code works locally. The platform team says deployment configuration is correct. The application repeatedly fails in production, and nobody considers the entire delivery path their responsibility.

### Planned Coverage

DevOps as an approach to collaboration and delivery; responsibilities of developers, infrastructure engineers, DevOps engineers, platform teams, and SREs; self-service platforms; environments; CI/CD workflows; infrastructure as code; GitOps; deployment ownership; secrets; observability; on-call responsibility; SLOs; incident response; and operational readiness. Distinguish platform engineering from SRE and from conventional infrastructure administration.

### Scope Boundaries

SE340 already covers CI/CD and Terraform mechanisms. SE450 covers Kubernetes and GitOps. SE440 covers reliability and SRE practices. This lecture connects the responsibilities and workflows rather than repeating their technical details.

### Expected Understanding

Explain what a DevOps/platform team actually provides, what a developer should own, and how to investigate a deployment problem collaboratively.

### Suggested Exercise / Lab

Design a responsibility map for an application progressing through build, testing, staging, production deployment, monitoring, and incident recovery.
