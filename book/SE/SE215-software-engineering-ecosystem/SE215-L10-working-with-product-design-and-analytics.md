# SE215 — The Software Engineering Ecosystem
## Lecture 10: Working With Product, Design, and Analytics

*A designer creates a beautiful booking flow, a developer implements it exactly, and the product manager declares the feature complete. Conversion nevertheless falls after release.*

Structure: Part I — Intuition, Part II — Deep Dive, oral examination, solutions, one Mental Model, Next Lecture hook; practical material after the spoken-end marker; Dense Cheatsheet at the end.

### Learning Objective

Understand how software developers participate in product outcomes beyond coding a completed specification.

### Opening Problem

A designer creates a beautiful booking flow, a developer implements it exactly, and the product manager declares the feature complete. Conversion nevertheless falls after release.

### Planned Coverage

Product discovery versus delivery; translating business goals into requirements; functional and nonfunctional requirements; user journeys; wireframes; prototypes; Figma and developer handoff; design systems; design tokens; responsive behavior; accessibility; component consistency; product analytics; funnels; conversion; retention; A/B testing; experimentation; feature rollout; and post-release learning.

### Scope Boundaries

Do not teach professional visual design, detailed Figma operation, or advanced statistical experimentation. Refer to CS120 for statistical reasoning and SE240 for frontend implementation.

### Expected Understanding

Translate a design and product objective into a deliverable feature, identify relevant measurements, and participate intelligently in discussions about usability and conversion.

### Suggested Exercise / Lab

Take a booking-flow redesign and construct a plan covering implementation, accessibility, release, instrumentation, and success measurement.
