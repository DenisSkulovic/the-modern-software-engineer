### Learning Objective

Develop a cloud-provider-neutral mental model of how ordinary applications are hosted and operated.

### Opening Problem

A startup wants to deploy a conventional SaaS application. Three consultants propose AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure. All claim their preferred platform is the obvious choice.

### Planned Coverage

IaaS, PaaS, serverless, managed services, shared responsibility, regions, availability zones, networking, identity, compute, object storage, managed databases, queues, observability, and pricing. Equivalent service categories in AWS, GCP, and Azure. Why comparing products by names alone can be misleading. Vendor lock-in, portability, regional availability, and cost surprises at a high level.

### Scope Boundaries

SE340-L03 already teaches AWS architecture in depth. Do not regenerate that chapter under a different title. This lecture is a conceptual provider comparison.

### Expected Understanding

Sketch how a conventional SaaS could be deployed on any of the three major providers and identify what the application team would still be responsible for.

### Suggested Exercise / Lab

Map a simple Node.js/PostgreSQL application to an AWS, GCP, and Azure architecture.
