## Lecture 01: The Art of Solving an Unfamiliar Problem

### Status — Planning Stub

Full voice-first manuscript not yet authored.

### Learning Objective

Establish a repeatable interview-solving process before learning specialized patterns.

### Opening Problem

An experienced backend developer understands the algorithm after seeing the solution but cannot discover it during an interview.

### Planned Coverage

Problem clarification, constraints, brute-force baselines, invariants, examples and counterexamples, complexity analysis, choosing data structures, testing edge cases, and communicating trade-offs.

### Scope Boundaries

CS110 teaches algorithmic ideas by listening. This course converts that knowledge into active problem solving under interview conditions — not a second tour of sorting theory.

### Expected Understanding

Walk through an unfamiliar problem with a clear process: clarify, baseline, improve, test, and narrate trade-offs out loud.

### Practice Focus

Short problems where reasoning and explanation matter more than implementation difficulty. Answers appear after the challenge attempt window.
