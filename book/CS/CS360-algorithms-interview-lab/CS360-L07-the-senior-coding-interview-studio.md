### Learning Objective

Deliver a credible engineering problem-solving process under time pressure.

### Opening Problem

A forty-five-minute interview includes an unfamiliar problem, uncertain requirements, implementation mistakes, and changing constraints.

### Planned Coverage

Integrated timed exercises, articulating assumptions, selecting a baseline, improving complexity, testing, explaining failure cases, handling interviewer hints, and communicating incomplete but sound reasoning.

### Scope Boundaries

Capstone studio across the course patterns — not a new algorithm family lecture.

### Expected Understanding

Complete mock interviews with a defensible process even when the optimal approach is not immediately obvious.

### Practice Focus

Several complete mock interviews, each with an evaluation rubric and model solution discussion after the attempt.
