### Learning Objective

Identify monotonic structure and explain why a proposed greedy solution is correct—or why it fails.

### Opening Problem

A scheduling problem appears to require examining every possible allocation, but a simple invariant eliminates most possibilities.

### Planned Coverage

Binary search on sorted data, binary search on a monotonic answer space, sorting as a preprocessing technique, interval problems, greedy choice, exchange arguments, and counterexamples to incorrect greedy strategies.

### Scope Boundaries

CS110 covers search and sorting foundations. This lecture trains recognition and correctness arguments for interview variants.

### Expected Understanding

Spot monotonic answer spaces; defend or refute a greedy choice with an exchange argument or counterexample.

### Practice Focus

Feasibility searches, intervals, resource allocation, and scheduling.
