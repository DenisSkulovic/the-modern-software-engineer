Not a distributed-systems course. Model business constraints as graphs; leave partial-failure and consensus to CS430.

### Expected Understanding

Model dependencies as graphs; choose DFS/BFS/topo/union-find appropriately; detect cycles and connected components.

### Practice Focus

Dependencies, grids, connected components, tree properties, shortest paths, and cycle detection.
