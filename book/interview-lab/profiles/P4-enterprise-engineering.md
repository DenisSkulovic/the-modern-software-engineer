# P4 — Established enterprise engineering team

**Status:** reusable preset  
**Use when:** Larger / more formal engineering organizations with structured hiring.

## Snapshot

| Field | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Company type | Enterprise or large established product org |
| Role | As in JD (often full-stack, backend, or platform) |
| Process feel | More formal / multi-stage |
| Algorithm-heavy | Depends on employer evidence |

## Prioritize

- Career history; cross-team collaboration; ownership
- Quality, reliability, design review, technical trade-offs
- Behavioral examples grounded in real work (not slogan STAR theatre)
- Coding or formal assessments only when the employer’s process indicates them

## Caution

“Enterprise” is not a synonym for “easy behavioral + hard leetcode.” Follow evidence.
