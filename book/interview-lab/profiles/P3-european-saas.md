# P3 — European SaaS / product engineering (structured)

**Status:** reusable preset  
**Use when:** European or Europe-based SaaS / product company with clearer product-engineering process than a scrappy early startup — still not “enterprise bureaucracy” (see P4).

## Snapshot

| Field | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Market | Europe / Europe-based international team (Germany, Netherlands, Sweden, or similar) |
| Company type | SaaS or product company (often scale-up or established product org) |
| Role | Senior full-stack or backend |
| Seniority | Senior unless JD says otherwise |
| Interview language | Usually English unless stated |
| Algorithm-heavy | Do not assume |

## Prioritize

- Requirements clarification; maintainability; API contracts
- Production quality; team collaboration; delivery judgment
- The job’s **actual** technology stack
- Cross-border / remote-friendly collaboration only when the company’s setup suggests it

## How this differs from P1

P1 is the broad default for practical EU product interviews.  
P3 leans harder into product-engineering craft (contracts, delivery, maintainability, collaboration) when that matches the JD — not into Israeli market habits, and not into LeetCode by default.

## Caution

This profile does **not** describe every European company or interviewer. Avoid national caricatures. Use P6 when a real invitation arrives.
